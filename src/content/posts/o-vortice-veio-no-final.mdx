Today I made a single cup with 10 grams of Dutra's Moka Peaberry and a 50 ml extraction.

I used 93 ml of whole milk. I did not notice much difference compared with semi-skimmed milk, but the smaller volume felt easier to steam.

The vortex began to form closer to the end. It felt like a sign that I had found a better position, even though I still lacked the steadiness to keep the movement consistent throughout the process.
