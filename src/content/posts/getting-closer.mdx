Moving one small step finer made the espresso rounder, with more chocolate and less of the thin finish from the previous attempt. The flow looked even and the puck came out clean.

I stretched the milk for too long. It looked glossy in the pitcher, but thickened quickly during the pour. The right texture feels close now; I just need to stop sooner.
