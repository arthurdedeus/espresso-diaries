Today I used 15 grams of Dutra's Moka Peaberry and extracted 171 ml of coffee in total. The idea was to make it deliberately gentler and divide it between two mugs.

I started with 160 ml of cold semi-skimmed milk. I managed to make some foam, but it did not integrate well with the rest of the milk and stayed concentrated on the surface.

When I poured, most of the foam only reached the coffee at the end and fell in all at once. In one of the mugs, the two white circles turned into a small snowman.
