The shot ran a touch faster than I expected, but it tasted more balanced than yesterday—soft acidity, a little honey, and no dry finish. I kept the milk moving gently and stopped stretching earlier.

The pour felt unhurried. The heart ended up small and slightly left of centre, but it stayed recognisable. That felt like progress.
